California Eats · honors are facts, not ratingsMICHELIN & James Beard in California

The MICHELIN Guide California 2026 selection and James Beard Award honors for California restaurants, as the guide and the Foundation announced them. 285 honored places are in the app, each with its own address and, where it has one, its official county inspection result.

Where these come from

MICHELIN distinctions are from MICHELIN's own announcement of the 2026 California selection (June 24, 2026). James Beard honors are from the James Beard Foundation's announcements, 2023 through 2026, plus California's America's Classics honorees from the Foundation's past-awards records since the program began. A finalist or semifinalist is always called that, never a winner. Places our research found closed since the announcement are left out. MICHELIN and the MICHELIN Guide are trademarks of Michelin; James Beard Foundation and James Beard Award are trademarks of the James Beard Foundation.
