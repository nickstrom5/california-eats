Privacy policy

Short version: this website doesn't track you. No account, no analytics, no ads, no cookies. Last updated November 19, 2026.

This website

The site is static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and loads no analytics, fonts or scripts from anyone else. The web app keeps three things in your browser's local storage, on your device only: the places you save (cae-saved), the last guide you opened (cae-guide) and the county you last picked under Inspections (cae-jur). Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes them. Its "Near me" button asks your browser for your location only when you press it; the location is used in the page to sort by distance and is never sent anywhere. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

The Eats Ranked app

On iPhone and iPad, the California guide is part of the Eats Ranked app, coming soon to the App Store. Eats Ranked has its own privacy policy at eatsranked.com/privacy.html. In short, it collects nothing about you. When it downloads or refreshes the California guide it fetches data files from this website, in requests that carry no location and no identifier; GitHub may log them as it does any visit here.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply and to fix the listing you told us about. We don't add you to a mailing list or share your address.

Children

This website collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

California residents

Because we collect no personal information through this site, there is nothing for us to sell, share or delete. If you emailed us and want that message deleted, ask at nick@eatsranked.com.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: nick@eatsranked.com.
